# Contributing

Contribute a focused case study, clearer diagram, correction, or reusable template.

## Content requirements

- Use fictional systems and synthetic data.
- Do not submit employer or customer information, private project details, credentials, internal URLs, or personal data.
- Distinguish assumptions, proposed controls, executed tests, and observed results.
- Connect threats to controls and verification procedures.
- Explain risk ratings and architecture tradeoffs.
- Attribute external material and confirm permission to share it.
- Do not claim certification, compliance, or measured effectiveness without evidence.

## Review checklist

- Does the model define scope and trust boundaries?
- Does each threat describe an actor, precondition, action, and consequence?
- Are controls tied to verification steps?
- Are remaining risks and evidence gaps explicit?
- Do relative links resolve?
- Are diagrams readable?
- Is all content appropriate for public release?

Open a pull request describing the problem, the change, and any validation performed.
